## Deliberate Deviations

- **[Topic / Subsystem]:** Why the implementation departs from the initial plan, what mitigation is in place, and what trigger will revisit it.

## Applied Review-Pass Hardening

- **[Date / Slice]:** Summary of defects, invariant gaps, or code smells caught during `decision-pr-review` and how they were resolved and tested.
